Cozy Logic Grid Puzzles

Gentle whodunits on a logic grid. No ads, no timers, offline, never a guess.

A quiet English village. A missing bake-off ribbon, a signed letter gone from a bookshop, a clock tower key that never reached the ceremony. Nobody is hurt, nothing is grim — but somebody was somewhere they should not have been, and the grid knows who.

Never a guess — and we mean it

Three things are checked by machine before a single case ships:

  • Exactly one solution. Not “we believe” — counted.
  • Not one clue you do not need. Every clue is tested for removal, and cut if the case survives without it.
  • You never have to guess. A puzzle can have exactly one answer and still be unreachable — you cross off everything the clues allow, and stop, with no forced next move. A second solver, restricted on purpose to what a person does with a pencil, has to finish every case before it is allowed in. If it stalls, the case is not shipped.

No ads. No timers. No internet.

There are no adverts and there will not be. Nothing is timed and nothing is scored against you. The whole app works offline because every case is already inside it, and it asks Android for no permissions at all.

On paper

The largest grids never fit a phone comfortably — that is what the books are for. Large print, room to write, no battery and no subscription.
